ABOUT Sibran Nkabinde Inc INC.

Built on experience.
Guided by principle.

A longstanding legal practice shaped by experience, professional commitment and a changing legal landscape.

Established on 4 July 2007 by Mr Anil Sukdew Sibran and Mr S. M. Nkabinde, Sibran Nkabinde Inc Inc. was founded to respond to the evolving demands of the legal profession and the needs of its clients.

Following Mr S. M. Nkabinde's departure due to a personal injury that prevented him from continuing as a partner, the firm continued under the leadership of Mr Anil Sibran.
